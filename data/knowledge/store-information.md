# Northstar Goods — store information

This is explicitly fictional sample business documentation for CustomerOps AI.
Effective date: 2026-10-01. Northstar Goods is an imaginary online-only home and workspace store.
No physical shop, collection counter, or real business is represented.

Human support is scheduled Monday through Friday, 09:00–17:00 UTC, excluding public holidays.
Support requests are normally reviewed within 2 business days. This is a target, not a service
guarantee. Opening a support ticket does not immediately connect a live agent.

The fictional contact address is support@northstar.example. The .example address is reserved
for documentation and cannot receive real customer email. Customers use the support-ticket
workflow in this demonstration. There is no telephone support number in the sample store.

The store uses USD. Demonstration checkout policies accept Visa and Mastercard credit or debit
cards; the backend does not process payments. Gift cards, installments, and cryptocurrency are
not supported in this fictional policy. Product prices and stock must come from live product
tools, not this document. Individual order facts must come from authenticated order tools.
