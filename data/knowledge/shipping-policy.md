# Northstar Goods — shipping policy

This is explicitly fictional sample business documentation for CustomerOps AI.
Effective date: 2026-10-01. Applies only to the demonstration store.

Northstar Goods ships within the contiguous United States only. International destinations,
Alaska, Hawaii, and PO boxes are not supported in this fictional policy.

Orders are normally processed within 2 business days. Business days are Monday through
Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed after 14:00 UTC begin processing on the
next business day. Processing time is separate from carrier transit time.

Standard shipping costs USD 6 and usually takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Standard
shipping is free for merchandise subtotals of USD 75 or more, before tax and after discounts.
Express shipping costs USD 15 and usually takes 1–2 business days after dispatch.
These are estimates, not delivery guarantees; severe weather or carrier disruptions can
cause delays. No same-day delivery service is offered.

A tracking reference is provided after dispatch. Ask the order-status tool for an individual
order's recorded status. Missing or damaged deliveries should be reported to support within
7 calendar days of the recorded delivery date. The assistant cannot change a delivery address.
