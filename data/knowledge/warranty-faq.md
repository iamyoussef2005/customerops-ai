# Northstar Goods — warranty FAQ

This is explicitly fictional sample business documentation for CustomerOps AI.
Effective date: 2026-10-01. Applies to this demonstration store only.

**How long is the warranty?** Northstar Goods provides a 12-month limited warranty from
the delivery date for manufacturing defects in its demonstration products.

**What is excluded?** Normal wear, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized repairs, and
consumable components are excluded. The warranty does not promise a lifetime replacement.

**How do I submit a claim?** Contact support with the order number, a description of the
problem, and clear photographs where possible. Keep the item until support gives instructions.
The assistant can open a support ticket but cannot authorize a warranty claim.

**What happens after approval?** Support chooses repair or replacement after assessment.
If neither is available, support may offer a refund. A particular remedy is not guaranteed
before assessment. Approved warranty shipping costs are covered by Northstar Goods.

**Does a replacement restart coverage?** A replacement keeps the original product's warranty
end date. This fictional policy does not describe or limit statutory consumer rights.
