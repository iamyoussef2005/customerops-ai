# Northstar Goods — return policy

This is explicitly fictional sample business documentation for CustomerOps AI.
Effective date: 2026-10-01. Applies to the fictional Northstar Goods demonstration store.

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery. Items must be
unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the order number. Personalized
items and opened hygiene products are excluded unless defective.

To request a return, contact support with the order number and reason. A support agent
must approve the request before the customer ships the item. The AI assistant can open
a support ticket but cannot approve returns or issue refunds.

For a change-of-mind return, the customer pays return postage. Northstar Goods covers
approved return postage for defective or incorrectly supplied items. Original shipping
charges are not refundable for change-of-mind returns.

After an approved return is received and inspected, a refund is initiated to the original
payment method within 5 business days. Banks may take additional time; no bank settlement
time is guaranteed. Exchanges require a separate return and a new purchase.
